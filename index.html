Some contractors deliver equipment. We deliver certainty — in every panel, every camera, every connection.

Auxiliary Wing Contracting is an engineering organization specialized and integrated in the technical services field — a genuine one-stop shop across electrical power (LV, MV & HV), CCTV & security systems, IT & communication, and maintenance & facility management, matching international requirements and standards.
