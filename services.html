With a clear focus on fast, friendly solutions and smooth-running efficiency across every project and operation — each capability below is led in-house, and signed for by name.

/ 01

Protection & Control Panels up to 230 kV

Turnkey supply of protection and control panels rated up to 230 kV, fully compliant with Saudi Electricity Company (SEC) and Saudi Aramco standards and specifications — from approved-vendor sourcing and project-specific scheme customisation through to factory testing and a complete QA/QC handover package.

  • Protection & control panels to 230 kV
  • SEC/Aramco-approved vendor sourcing
  • Project-specific scheme customisation
  • Panel integration & internal wiring
  • Factory acceptance testing (FAT)
  • Technical submittals & QA/QC packages
/ 02

Medium Voltage Solutions

Supply, installation, testing and commissioning of medium-voltage distribution equipment. Integrated with remote terminal units (RTU) and innovative condition-monitoring sensors, the smart and motorised ring main units are all-in-one solutions — easy to purchase, install and maintain, reducing operational cost, with a robust design that fits even the harshest environments.

  • Medium voltage switchgear
  • Smart Ring Main Units (RMU)
  • Motorised Ring Main Units (MRMU)
  • RTU & condition-monitoring integration
  • Installation, testing & commissioning
  • Design submission & as-built delivery
/ 03

Low Voltage Panel Solutions

Supply, installation, testing and commissioning of low-voltage panels — a complete turnkey solution integrating engineering, procurement and execution. Whether for industrial, commercial or infrastructure projects, our technical team guarantees precise installation and thorough testing against every operational and safety standard.

  • LV distribution panels
  • Automatic transfer switch (ATS) panels
  • Motor control centres (MCC)
  • Vendor procurement & compliance
  • Installation & commissioning
  • Operational & safety testing
/ 04

Power Protection Engineering

Engineering services for power protection systems, prepared exactly to SEC, National Grid and Saudi Aramco standards: schematic drawings per SEEDS, OPDS, interconnection diagrams, cable schedules and component selection per PTS & SOW — followed by SDMS, TMSS & SAMSS requirements.

  • Protection schematics (SEEDS)
  • OPDS preparation
  • Interconnection diagrams
  • Cable schedules
  • Component selection per PTS & SOW
  • SDMS, TMSS & SAMSS compliance
/ 05

Access Control & CCTV Systems

Intelligent access control and CCTV with the latest technology and highest quality — full project scope from design and supply through technical support, installation, testing & commissioning, and third-party approval where required. Backed by the best maintenance team for after-sales service.

  • System design & consultancy
  • CCTV supply & installation
  • Intelligent access control
  • Testing & commissioning
  • Third-party approvals
  • After-sales maintenance
/ 06

IT, Networking & Communications

The digital backbone for power sectors and process industries: IT infrastructure, enterprise networking, telecommunication services and professional services — designed, deployed and supported alongside leading global technology partners.

  • IT infrastructure & supply
  • Enterprise networking
  • Telecommunication services

  • Managed & professional services
  • Helpdesk & support

Cyber Security

Professional security services that protect what powers you — from perimeter to endpoint, audit to certification. Delivered with partners including Sophos, Fortinet, Bitdefender, Trend Micro and WatchGuard.

  • Penetration testing

  • Phishing simulation
  • Security & infrastructure audits
  • Security gap assessment
  • Aramco certification support
  • Firewalls, endpoint & email security
  • Zero trust, SD-WAN & WAF
