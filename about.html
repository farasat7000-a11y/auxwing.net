The Story

Engineered in the field,
refined by standards.

Right Tech International is an engineering organization specialized and integrated in the technical services field — a leading one-stop-shop across electrical power (LV, MV & HV), CCTV & security systems, IT & communication, and maintenance & facility management, matching international requirements and standards.

The success of the house stems from a positive and dynamic approach: advanced technology, a natural aptitude for innovation and optimized solutions, and a highly qualified, dedicated team of technical and administrative staff — backed by extensive resources of proficient manpower and modern equipment.

Our agenda is simple: provide the requisite technical services to every client, using the latest equipment and technology — and remain a real, supportive partner long after commissioning.

The Standard

What we refuse to negotiate.

Standards before shortcuts

SEC, National Grid and Saudi Aramco standards — SEEDS, OPDS, SDMS, TMSS & SAMSS — govern every schematic, cable schedule and component we select. No exceptions.

One accountable hand

Design, supply, installation, testing & commissioning and third-party approval — one integrated team, one point of responsibility, one standard of finish.

Care after commissioning

"Powering Tomorrow with Excellence" is not a slogan for the proposal stage. Our maintenance team keeps every system at handover standard — with 24/7 after-sales response.
